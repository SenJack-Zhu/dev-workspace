**NativeTavern v0.1.13 (iOS Build 34)**
**Changes since v0.1.12 (Build 30)**

**Privacy and data controls**
• Added clear consent before chat, prompt, character, persona, or conversation context is sent to the remote AI provider selected by the user.
• The consent screen identifies the destination, including OAI Compatible endpoints, and declining prevents remote AI requests.
• Added localized consent messaging across all supported app languages.

**Chat and provider fixes**
• Fixed iPad overflow menus closing immediately after they were opened.
• Fixed group-chat avatars and names, chat loading, personal-chat isolation, and Author's Note persistence.
• Fixed duplicated Gemini streaming output and improved Groq Compound compatibility.
• Fixed Google Drive sign-in configuration on Android.

**Availability**
• iOS: Build 34 is available for internal TestFlight testing; App Store version 1.2.13 has been submitted for review.
• Android: Version 34 has been submitted to Google Play production.
• Direct APK: https://download.nativetavern.com/NativeTavern.apk
